PRICINGPILOT — PORTABLE REVIEW

An offline quote calculator for a small service business.
This review does not start a subscription or send any information to TechPartner.
Prices come from your cost inputs and local formulas, not a live AI service.

OPEN IT
1. Extract the whole ZIP into a folder you plan to keep. Do not open the app
   from inside the ZIP, and keep its css, js and lib folders together.
2. Right-click index.html, choose Open with, then Microsoft Edge.
   No installer, account, API key, terminal or administrator access is needed.
3. Wait for "Editing tab." before saving. If you see View-only, close any other
   copy at this path and use Reload latest data. If policy or storage blocks
   access, stop and tell TechPartner; do not disable security protections.

YOUR FIRST PRACTICE QUOTE — USE MADE-UP DATA
1. Estimate costs: enter 1 labor hour, $100 hourly cost, $0 materials,
   $0 other costs, 0% overhead and 40% target margin. Click Calculate.
   The recommended price should be $166.67. This is margin, not markup.
2. Save it with the name "Practice quote". Reload the page, then load that
   saved quote. The hourly cost should still be $100.
3. Build tiers: review each price and its actual scope. Price multipliers do
   not add extra materials or labor to your costs. Edit the inclusions yourself.
4. Present quote: use a made-up business and job name. Leave internal profit
   margins OFF, render the quote, and review it before printing or saving a PDF.
   The customer copy should not show your internal margin table.
5. Return to Estimate costs. Open Backup & restore, then Download full backup.
   Check that the JSON file exists in Downloads. Keep a copy outside this app
   folder. A CSV export is a summary, not a full backup.
6. Choose the JSON file in Backup & restore. Review the preview, keep Merge,
   and apply. Your practice quote should not be duplicated. Retain the requested
   pre-restore recovery download too. Do not use Replace for this first check.

BEFORE USING REAL BUSINESS INFORMATION
- Tell TechPartner whether these six checks worked and which browser you used.
  This package remains a review candidate until your launch and workflow work.
- Keep this folder at a stable path and use the same browser/profile. Data is
  stored in that browser, not in the ZIP. Moving folders, switching profiles or
  clearing browsing data may separate you from saved records.
- Keep regular JSON backups and customer PDFs in your own secure records.
  Backups include private cost information. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
- Review scope, costs, tax assumptions, tier promises and the final PDF yourself.
  Won quote totals are estimates, not payments received. There is no invoicing,
  card charging, bank integration or shared team database.

NEED HELP?
Report the step number, the visible error and your Edge/Windows version through
your existing TechPartner conversation. Do not send passwords, API keys or real
customer files just to report a problem. Read USER-GUIDE.txt for recovery details.

UPDATING OR REMOVING
Download and verify a full JSON backup before any update or move. Keep old
folders and backups until the new copy is verified. Removing extracted files
does not remove browser records. No setup/uninstall script is included.
